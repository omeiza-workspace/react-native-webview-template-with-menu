GE-Fix is a modern service management app designed to streamline issue tracking, reporting, and resolution for users and service teams.

Key features include real-time updates, task tracking, and a simple, intuitive interface.

GE-Fix is your native iOS companion app for managing service requests efficiently. With GE-Fix, you can:

- Navigate quickly with a native tab bar for core features
- Receive real-time push notifications for updates and alerts
- Work offline with a custom offline screen and retry mechanism
- Upload photos directly using your device camera
- Enjoy smooth, app-like transitions without page reloads

GE-Fix combines the best of web content with iOS-native performance and functionality, providing an experience beyond a mobile browser.
